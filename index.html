01 Overview

BuddyVerse is a party-game app — jokes, riddles, trivia, and about 25 mini-games meant to be played with the person next to you. This policy explains, plainly, what the app collects, why, and how you can ask us to delete it.

Most of BuddyVerse works with nothing collected at all: no account, no sign-up, no login screen in your way. The two things below only happen if you choose to use them.

02 What we collect

Nothing required to playNo adsNo trackers
  • Email address Optional. Only collected if you choose to create an account from the app's Log In screen, used to manage that account. Skipping it doesn't limit any gameplay.
  • Feedback & reports Optional. If you tap Feedback or Report on a game screen, the message you type — and which game it's about — is sent to us so we can read and act on it.
  • First name Optional. If you tap "Get Updates" and choose to sign up, we store the first name you enter (no email, no last name) so we can show you in-app notices when a new version or feature comes out. Skipping the name field still lets you opt in.
  • Multiplayer session data Not personal. Two-player online games relay room codes and moves between devices while a match is active. This is anonymous game state, not tied to your identity.

03 How we use it

Email address is used only to manage the account you created with it. Feedback and report text is read by the app's developer to fix bugs, improve games, and respond to concerns. A first name entered through "Get Updates" is used only to personalize in-app update notices — it's never emailed, texted, or used to contact you outside the app. Multiplayer session data exists only to sync a live match between two devices and isn't used for anything beyond that.

We don't use anything you send us to build advertising profiles, and we don't sell data — there's nothing here structured for that, and it stays that way.

04 Sharing & third parties

BuddyVerse stores data on Google's Firebase platform (Authentication and Firestore), which acts strictly as our infrastructure — it processes data on our behalf, under our instruction, to run the app's backend. It is not handed to Firebase, or anyone else, for their own separate use.

We don't share, sell, or rent your information to any other third party. There are no ad networks or analytics SDKs in BuddyVerse collecting data on their own behalf.

05 Security

Everything sent to or from BuddyVerse travels encrypted, over standard HTTPS/TLS connections via Firebase. Feedback and report content is readable only by the app's admin account.

06 Retention & deletion

We keep account, feedback, and report data only as long as it's useful for the purposes above. You can ask us to delete anything tied to you at any time — reach out using the contact details below and we'll take care of it.

07 Children's privacy

BuddyVerse includes a "Kid" content mode built for younger players, and it collects nothing beyond what's described above for anyone, of any age — no ads, no behavioral tracking, no data sold. The "Get Updates" opt-in deliberately asks only for a first name, never an email address, so it stays safe for younger players too. A parent or guardian who wants any information deleted can use the contact details below.

08 Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continuing to use BuddyVerse after a change means you accept the current version.

09 Contact

Questions, concerns, or a deletion request — reach the BuddyVerse team at buddyversemithran@gmail.com, the same address listed in the app's Contact Us screen.
