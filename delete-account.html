01 Overview

BuddyVerse, developed by Mithran ObliPrabu, doesn't have a traditional sign-up or account system for players — but if you've entered a first name in the app, opted into update notices, created a real Log In account, or sent us feedback, that data lives in our database and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

There's no in-app "delete my account" button today, since there's no traditional account to delete — instead, you request deletion directly from us using the steps below, and we take care of it by hand.

02 What can be deleted

First nameLogin emailFeedback & reports
  • First name Whatever you entered on first launch, and/or separately through the "Get Updates" opt-in.
  • Login email Only exists if you created a real account from the app's Log In screen.
  • Feedback & reports Any message you submitted through a game screen's Feedback or Report button.

03 How to request deletion

  1. Email us

    Send a message to buddyversemithran@gmail.com asking to delete your BuddyVerse data.

  2. Tell us how to find your data

    Include the first name you used in the app, and roughly when you played. If you created a real Log In account, include that email address too — this is how we find the right records, since the app doesn't use any other account identifier.

  3. We confirm once it's done

    We'll reply once your data has been deleted from our database, usually within a few days.

04 What happens next

Once we process your request, your first name, login email (if any), and any feedback/report messages tied to you are permanently removed from our Firestore database. Anonymous data that was never tied to your identity in the first place — like live multiplayer session state, which only exists for the duration of an active match — isn't retained at all and has nothing to delete.
